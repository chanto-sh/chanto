- **Edits no longer change text around them.** When an edit's `old_string` matched only after ignoring whitespace, the replacement was pasted in as written: a first line could lose its indentation (breaking Python), a blank line could appear, and tabs or CRLF line endings were replaced with spaces and LF. The replacement is now fitted to the file's indentation and line endings, and when its indentation can't be placed for certain the edit is refused with the matched lines so the model can retry exactly.
